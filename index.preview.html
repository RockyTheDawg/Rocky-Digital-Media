About Cap'n Blox & M.E.G.

Cap'n Blox's Official Site hosts the Materials & Exploration Group (M.E.G.) developer resources for Backrooms projects, documentation, and community archives. The goal is to provide reliable tooling and canonical references for contributors and curious developers.
